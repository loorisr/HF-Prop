About

Monthly-median predictions with the ITU-R P.533-14 method (ITU-R Study Group 3 reference code, ITURHFProp v14.3), compiled to WebAssembly and run in your browser — no server. Real conditions vary from day to day (storms, sporadic-E, aurora): treat this as a statistical forecast.

Engine & data: ITU-R SG3 (Behm, Engelbrecht, Watson). WASM port inspired by OpenHamClock. Solar data: NOAA SWPC. Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO.
